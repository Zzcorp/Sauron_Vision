## How to read it

- **Rank.** By how often he sees it (every page, then often, sometimes, rarely); inside a tier, his most used surfaces first; a money control first among equals.
- **Money.** "yes" when the element is a §10 row of the plan (a control that moves money, a PIN, a world marker, a price or P&L cell) or sits inside one.
- **Batch.** The pull request that removes it (`batches.md`). "x-cut" rows are cross-cutting counts that every batch shrinks; B24's guard holds them at zero.
- **Raw.** The sweep items merged into the row.
- The UI plan's rules are cited by section: §2.2 the ten rules, §3 palette, §4 type and numbers, §5 shape, §6 shell, §7 kit, §8 motion (binding), §9 icons, §10 money controls (binding), §11 live hooks (binding).
- Every element below was seen on synthetic data only.
