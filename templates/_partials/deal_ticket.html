{% load sauron_tags %} {% comment %} The deal ticket — SHORT at the bid, the spread, LONG at the ask. The price rides the button that trades at it. LONG lifts the ASK and SHORT hits the BID, so pairing them by eye was work the operator was doing at the exact moment they were committing money. Used in two places and identical in both: the instrument header, and an overlay on the expanded chart. `data-dq` are the live hooks — sv- instrument-live.js repaints EVERY one of them on the page, which is why two tickets can coexist without either going stale. symbol — required, the instrument symbol quote — the LiveQuote (may be None; the ticket degrades to em-dashes) klass — extra classes, e.g. "sv-ticket--sm" decimals — how many places prices carry, for the live repaint A missing side renders the muted em-dash, never a zero: the house rule is that unknown is not a number, and it matters most here, where the number sits on a button that opens a position. {% endcomment %}
