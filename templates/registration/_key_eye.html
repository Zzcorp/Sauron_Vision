{% comment %} THE EYE ON THE KEY (2026-09-28). The operator: "un petit œil vert à l'image de Sauron pour pouvoir voir son mot de passe tapé". A button at the right end of a password field that shows what was typed while it is pressed on, and hides it again on the second press. The eye is the mark's own eye (static/logo/sauron_eye.svg) reduced to a 26x16 glyph and painted in currentColor, so its state is a colour, not a second drawing: shut and dim in the label's grey while the key is hidden, open and lit in the accent while it is shown. Two groups, one visible at a time; the lid band is the mark's evenodd vesica so it keeps its weight at 16 px, and the pupil is the ground colour knocked out of the iris. Included with: {% include "registration/_key_eye.html" with target="id_password" what="access key" %} `target` is the id of the password input the eye reveals; the input must be wrapped in
with the include right after it. `what` is the noun the accessible name uses ("Show the access key"). Never a submit: type="button", and the form leaving (submit, or the page going to the background on a phone that switches apps) hides the key again, so a revealed key is never the state a page is left in. {% endcomment %}
