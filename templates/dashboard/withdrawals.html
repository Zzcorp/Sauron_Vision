What a request does, and what it does not
A request filed here holds the amount back from the moment it is filed. Every pool that follows the account is sized from the account less the reserve, so all of them shrink by the same proportion; the eToro cash gate will not spend the reserved cash on a new order.
It sells nothing and closes nothing. The pools' shares of the account do not change, the bots keep trading their plan, and cash builds up as positions close on their own.
When the money is sent at the broker, press Mark withdrawn at that moment: the reserve is released and the account's history counts the amount as a withdrawal, not as a loss, so no drawdown alarm and no de-risking follow from it. If the amount or the time was wrong, correct it in the history below — both numbers change how the account's past reads.
Not reduced by a reserve: live pools whose capital was typed by hand (listed below). Never affected: paper pools. Every action on this page takes the trading PIN.
