The denominator is the capital assigned to the ENABLED configs that can FILL on that venue — the same pool each entry's own risk fraction divides by — so the desk's ceiling and the per-order ceiling are denominated in the same money. LIVE is the live-mode configs only. PAPER is every enabled config, live-mode ones included: a rule with no promotion record, or still at the paper stage, is forced onto the paper venue whatever its config's mode says, so a live config's capital backs paper fills too. A live config therefore appears in BOTH denominators, and that is correct — these are two separate books, never summed and never netted, and only the live one stands in front of real money. Paper and live never share a budget, a book or a correlation penalty. The open book includes hand-taken positions: they bypass the desk and they are still money at risk.
