What a personality is — and the one correction about leverage

A personality is not a new engine. Every knob it needs already decides how a bot behaves; what was missing is that they were set one at a time with no coherence between them, graded over one 90-day window that fits none of them, and allocated out of one 2–60% band. A persona is therefore four things and nothing more: a coherent preset of knobs that already exist, a grading window matched to its holding period, a share band in the account allocator, and a weight on the 5–10 year horizon prior.

No personality borrows to fund a position. The only config a personality can be worn by carries no leverage and no margin field at all, so there is nothing there for a persona to set. The claim is deliberately scoped to personalities: a sweeping one about the whole codebase would be false, because the dormant futures engine carries both a leverage and a margin-mode field. The honest short-term lever is the notional fraction the risk sizer is allowed to reach — how much position one fixed cash risk budget buys when the stop is tight — and the number of concurrent positions. On every asset class but one, both are cash and neither is a loan. Forex is the exception: the notional cap there stays at 4.0 — 400% of the pool — and that leverage is carried by the broker’s CFD margin, not by this code. A persona changes no capital and no account share by itself, and never enables or disables a bot. Applying one to a live config re-sizes real risk on the next entry, which is why the form below asks for the trading PIN.

How to read this page

A number appears only when a grade exists: — means unmeasured, which is a different fact from zero, and a lane needs {{ min_n }} graded fills before the platform calls it measured. Paper and live are never pooled — the gap between them is exactly what the grading exists to measure. Each persona is graded over its own window, so a scalp book is judged on 21 days and a position book on 365. Personalities cover the {{ asset_classes }} classes; options price their stops off premium and carry their own expiry gate, so an ATR preset would describe a trade that class never takes. The same three presets, the same numbers and the same warnings are available from the shell: python manage.py persona list, python manage.py persona show scalp, python manage.py persona grade.
