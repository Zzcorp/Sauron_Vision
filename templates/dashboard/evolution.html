How a mutation is judged
1 mutate 1–3 constants inside the schema bounds → 2 replay parent and mutant over train and test → 3 score = backtest parent mean + min(Δtrain, Δtest) → 4 a fork trades beside its parent, never over it
▲ ROBUST both halves improved ◆ OVERFIT train up, test down ▼ WORSE measured, and it lost {# ◌ is the platform's declared "nothing measured" mark. ⚠ reads as a #} {# warning about the RESULT, which is the exact misreading this key #} {# exists to prevent: there is no result, only an absent measurement. #} ◌ NOT MEASURED below the trade floor ○ NO BACKTEST heuristic placeholder

The number that gets misread. A mutation needs at least {{ min_trades }} trades on every one of the four legs. Miss that on any leg and no delta is computed at all — the row is stamped with the backtest parent's mean plus a flat {{ penalty|floatformat:2 }}R placeholder, and when the parent's own legs came back empty there is no mean under it either. That score is a marker for untested, not a result. Cards in that state are drawn dashed and gold, and their deltas stay as em-dashes, because there is nothing there to print.

This is the expected reading on a young install, not a fault. Nothing here is hidden and nothing is pending.

  • A rule needs a registered schema. {{ n_evolvable }} of {{ n_families }} families have one; the rest are left alone by design and are listed below with the reason.
  • A rule has to be decaying. The proposer only mutates rules the decay check has flagged — a rule that is working is not a question worth asking.
  • The sweep is evidence-paced. It runs Sundays at 05:00 UTC without condition, on other days only when the week's closed trades are dense enough to mean something, and immediately whenever the nightly 02:30 UTC investigator confirms decay.

When one lands it appears here as a card: which constants moved and how far inside their bounds, the four walk-forward legs behind the score, and {{ ttl_days }} days to decide before it expires and is asked again.
