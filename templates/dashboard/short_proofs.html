What the platform proves by itself
For every class whose short no code proves, the platform sells the venue minimum on eToro's demo account, reads the fill, closes the position and reads the close — at 1x, then 2x and 5x where eToro lists them, and once per class it holds a short across the close to measure its overnight fee. Its client reaches the demo account only, whatever the eToro row's demo flag says.
A clean round trip lets the bots short that class at that multiplier and no other, behind every other gate. The cost filter then charges the measured short fee, never less than before.
