TO GANDALF

KEEPER OF THE HOUSE · OPENED {{ opened_at|date:"j F Y" }}, 4 PM, PARIS

Dad, if you are reading this, I have left and the house is yours. Sauron is running, the guards are watching, and the only hand that counts for the next three weeks, at least, is yours.

First I want to thank you, and to say it properly, here, where you will spend your mornings. Thank you for the hours already given: the evenings watching me lose my temper with a machine, the questions that landed just when I thought I was done, the patience in front of a terminal that owed you nothing. A good part of what Sauron knows how to refuse, it knows because you asked "what if?" at the right moment. That is not nothing. It is the heart of it.

And thank you for what came before. If I could spend months building a tower that sees far, it is because a house stood behind me, long before Sauron. You were the one holding it up. The most precious thing Sauron knows how to do, keep a cool head and stop in time, I learned from you before I ever wrote it into a machine. They say a wizard is never late. You have always been there on time.

Then, to tell you plainly what I am leaving in your care. Not money to grow: a house to keep. The job is not to win, it is to break nothing. Sauron knows how to stop on its own; Morgul's guards cut the bots at the first serious sign; the alarm bot will only speak to you about a real problem, and its silence is good news. Your vigilance is worth more than any heroic move. If in doubt, you stop everything with one word, and you write to me. I will never hold it against you for stopping: here, stopping is doing it right.

Finally, to tell you I am leaving with peace of mind, and that it is thanks to you. Three weeks is long for a machine and short for a man; I know you will hold both.

Thank you, Dad. For the watch, and for everything else. Keep the house, Gandalf. I will be back.

YOUR SON, PROUD TO BEWritten the day before leaving · Sauron Vision

THE WATCH, IN PRACTICE

  • Every morning, five minutes. Open /health/: every line green, or explained. Then look at the alarm Telegram group: silence is the good news.
  • Two words, and nothing else, in the alarm group. /status says what is running, how many bots, how many positions, and the critical problems of the moment. /stopall is the brake: every bot OFF, pending orders withdrawn, no position closed. They stay OFF until someone re-arms them on the server.
  • The Eye's group is the trading chatter (signals, fills, digests). You can mute it without missing anything important: what matters goes through the alarm.
  • When to write to your son: the alarm bot has spoken; a red line on /health/ for more than an hour; a message you do not understand. When in doubt, /stopall first, the message second.
Never do: "All on" next to System; touch the keys on /brokers/; arm a live config; close a position by hand at the broker without going through the platform; type a command in the terminal that your son has not written out in black and white.

THE SECOND BOT, IN FIVE MOVES

The alarm bot is a second Telegram bot in a second group of three: you, your son, and it. If it is already in place, this page is a reminder. If not, here is how it is born.

  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot, give it a name ("Morgul") and a username ending in bot. It answers with a token: that is a key, never to be copied into a chat.
  2. Create a new group with your son, you, and this bot, and no one else. Send a first message in it (any message).
  3. On the server, put the token in .env (TELEGRAM_ALARM_BOT_TOKEN), then ./deploy/dc up -d. Read the group's id with manage.py alarm --chats and put it in TELEGRAM_ALARM_CHAT_ID, then ./deploy/dc up -d again.
  4. Prove delivery: manage.py alarm --test sends a single message to the group. If it does not arrive, nothing is switched on.
  5. Switch on: manage.py component on telegram_alarm. From then on, the group's silence is the good news, and /status answers there.
