{% comment %} The venue is only half the answer, and the missing half is what made a seeded install look broken: an operator armed a strategy, watched nothing happen, and nothing anywhere named the second switch. {% endcomment %}

A venue is only half of it. Two switches decide whether a strategy does anything at all, and a freshly seeded one arrives with both off: the setup's is_active flag is what the scanner reads before it evaluates a single condition, and the rule's stage is what the bot reads before it places an order. A silent new strategy is not a broken one — arming it is what starts the evidence, and only that evidence moves it up a venue. Every card below names the switches still holding it and the action that clears each.
